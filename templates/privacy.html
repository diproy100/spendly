Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored only in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details, bank credentials, or any information from your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to record and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the database that powers the service. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including hashing passwords and limiting access to the systems that hold it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you may ask us to delete your account and its data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may make requests to Google's servers when you visit the site. Apart from this, we do not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your data is handled, or if you would like your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example. We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the date at the top of this page when we do.
